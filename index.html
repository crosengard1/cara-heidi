What is the main point of this screen?

Meet every requirement without giving every requirement equal space. Put the core task first; make supporting information easy to reach.

One compact context area
Identify the patient and care period once. Avoid repeating them in a header, card and details block.

Primary content in the first screenful
Encounters show sessions. Notes show notes. Work shows tasks.

Available does not mean always expanded
Use details, sheets and inline expansion. Keep verification-critical identity accessible.

Core journey: Today or Patients → Encounter → Scribe / Session → Note → Suggested action → Work. Ad hoc Scribe can start immediately and be organised later.

1. Today: appointments and round

P2Mostly aligned

Requirements: Stories 1–2

Keep the Appointments/Round split, direct encounter entry and working ward filter. Add an explicit non-EHR empty state and a clear Add appointment / Add inpatient care path. Represent manual-only, EHR-only and mixed lists with accurate source labels. Demonstrate active, future and ended care so Today’s inclusion rules are visible. The live demo is seeded with Cerner data; manual forms exist, but the complete first-use journey was not submitted in this audit.

2. Patient directory

P1Needs structural update

Requirements: Stories 2–3

Replace today/round grouping with an alphabetical, searchable directory of current and historical patients. Keep a compact identifier and useful care metadata; use active care as a secondary label, not the organising principle. Retain the lightweight Add patient flow. Add edit access and demonstrate a patient with no encounters. The existing create form only collects name and date of birth.

3. Patient profile and continuity

P1Major requirement gap

Requirements: Stories 3–5, 8

Turn Patient history into a usable profile: identity, concise clinician-curated context, accessible persistent details, and encounters. Include medical history, medications, allergies and notes without placing a large form on the landing view. Add the green Scribe shortcut beside the patient name. Add access to available files/documents with encounter provenance. Use the requested Encounter label and consistent nature + patient + date titles; remove generic history explanations.

View captured screens

4. Encounter overview and hierarchy

P1Aligned model; excessive detail before useful content

Requirements: Stories 4–5, 10

Preserve Patient → Encounter → Session for all encounters. Compress repeated patient/title/type information and administrative metadata so session history is visible sooner. Keep one clear Scribe action and patient link; replace duplicate View patient history / Explore history paths with one consistent entry. Show a legible appointment/inpatient type, start/end or open period, status and separate Heidi/EHR IDs. An EHR linked badge currently supplies no actual EHR encounter ID. Related outputs need a place when represented; task placement remains an open decision.

View captured screens

5. Scribe entry and capture

P1Major requirement gap

Requirements: Stories 5–6

Patient-page one-action Scribe is missing. Provide that shortcut with automatic new encounter creation and optional setup/linking that never blocks capture. Capture currently puts introductory text and a context card ahead of a small recording control near the bottom. Use the familiar central green recording action, compact identity/context and accessible optional setup. Add the recording/dictation choice. Preserve ad hoc capture and the same encounter context when entering from an encounter. This audit inspected the ready state; it did not start a recording or create a session.

View captured screens

6. Assigning and correcting context

P1Contradicts required model

Requirements: Story 6

Remove both Keep at patient level for now and Remove encounter link when the patient remains assigned. Assigning a patient must create/select an encounter, without requiring capture to stop for setup. Distinguish changing the patient from changing that patient’s encounter; show clear type, date, ID and status in choices. Define the later EHR-association flow and what happens to the automatically created encounter. Preserve source links and derived-task context. Assignment screens were opened but existing sessions were not reassigned.

7. Session browsing, note and details

P2Latest note layout substantially improved

Requirements: Stories 6–8

Keep the refreshed note-first layout, Copy note control and secondary Details sheet. Do not rebuild the former large context card. Add access to transcript and session context/files, plus a clear review/edit state once the minimum note workflow is agreed. Make Sessions searchable by session/patient/context instead of routing its search to patients only. Replace ambiguous New session / Today · just now labels with meaningful titles and timestamps. Include distinct no-note, processing, unavailable and expired examples where relevant; do not invent retention durations. EHR delivery is excluded.

View captured screens

8. Work and accepting suggestions

P1Good layout; missing state transition

Requirements: Stories 9–10

Keep To do / Work in progress / Done, inline detail expansion, ownership and source links. Add a lightweight review point for suggestions, either after the session or in a compact Suggestions queue. Add to Work must be separate from the completion checkbox and from asking Heidi to execute it. Current suggested items already sit in To do with a completion checkbox. Once accepted, the item belongs in To do until acted upon. Do not add a 24-hour countdown until expiry is decided. For supported Heidi work, represent ready-for-review, failed and completed states without implying that producing a draft completes the requested external action.

View captured screens

9. Manual creation and encounter lifecycle

P2Useful forms; incomplete lifecycle

Requirements: Stories 1, 4, 9–10

Keep standalone patient, encounter and task creation. Generate a consistent encounter title from care details instead of relying on an arbitrary mandatory name. Allow care-period metadata needed by the model, including optional end, and explain open care. Add a defined ending/status flow after lifecycle rules are resolved. A completed encounter currently disables Start session but is still offered in the assignment picker; that difference needs an intentional rule and explanation. For task context, replace the long all-encounters dropdown with a searchable picker and sensible current-context default.

View captured screens

10. Ask Heidi and search

P2Entry points work; workflow demonstration incomplete

Requirements: Story 11; cross-app usability

Keep Ask Heidi global and retain useful current-screen context. Add a few clearly simulated examples that demonstrate find/summarise → inspect source and request work → track in Work. A typed request should have a visible outcome in the prototype rather than only explanatory text. Search opened from Work currently says Find work but only lists patients. Use search scoped to the current area, or a clearly labelled global search with grouped results. Do not claim live agent execution or EHR integration.

11. Retention and unavailable content

Decision requiredMissing designed states; policy open

Requirements: Stories 7–8

Demonstrate the difference between a patient with no sessions, an encounter whose session content expired, and explicitly retained patient details. Explain unavailable content without implying that all history is permanent. Keep provenance when showing session-derived content on a patient page. The current policy for audio, transcripts, notes, attachments, tasks and encounter metadata has not been verified, so this report does not prescribe a retention period or claim actual deletion behaviour.

12. Accessibility and screen density

P1 focus / P2 polishConfirmed focus issue; visual risks need measurement

Requirements: All stories

Opening Add new and pressing Tab focused the underlying Go back button, outside the dialog. Move focus into every sheet, keep background content inert, contain keyboard navigation and restore focus on close. The refreshed Session details sheet does move focus to Close, so reuse its behaviour consistently. Make selected filters/navigation programmatically clear. Small muted metadata and pale task circles deserve contrast and target-size checks. The floating Ask Heidi/navigation dock obscures content during scrolling; provide a clear lower boundary/safe area and keep essential actions above it. Full screen-reader, contrast, zoom, virtual-keyboard and native-device tests were not run.

Notion inconsistencies to reconcile

The older tables still prescribe separate To-dos / Agent work views, a single-session appointment reading as one page, and + starting an ad hoc session. The later stories and confirmed discussion support the grouped Work list, preserving the encounter hierarchy and standalone creation. Keep the latest agreed experience and update the older wording, rather than redesigning to match both. Later EHR linking is desired behaviour with unresolved mechanics, not a verified integration capability.

Evidence limits

Reviewed navigation, lists, ward filter, patient/encounter/session detail, creation and assignment sheets, task expansion, Ask Heidi, search and keyboard focus. No new records were submitted, recordings started, tasks completed or existing records reassigned. Save persistence, duplicate detection, retention enforcement, actual AI/EHR behaviour, clipboard transfer and note editing were not verified. No warnings/errors were observed in the tab logs. This is an audit of designed states and interactions, not a backend or clinical validation.
