    If session content expires

    This is a discussion scenario, not a deletion preview. Explicitly saved patient information is intended to persist independently. Retention of encounter metadata, documents, tasks and receipts is unresolved. No automatic cascade is assumed, and no retention duration has been set.

    The screens are views

    Today brings relevant encounters, tasks and routine outputs together. Patients surfaces patient history and care lists. Sessions includes unassigned captures. Work tracks actions.

    A round is a worklist across patients’ inpatient encounters. It is not a shared encounter.

    Decisions still to make

    • Document ownership and patient-level task links.
    • Encounter defaults, corrections and lifecycle.
    • Retention and deletion rules for each record.
    • EHR matching, field ownership and saving patients.

    Routine execution and encounter-level outputs are later scope.
